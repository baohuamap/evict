# Workspace Organization

- `main.tex`, `sections/`, `references.bib`, `figures/`: manuscript source and paper assets
- `data/literature_exports/`: CSV exports from Scholar, SciSpace, arXiv, PubMed, and merged tables
- `docs/research_notes/`: literature notes, proposal drafts, theory notes, and parsing/reference notes
- `docs/reviews/`: reviewer reports, meta-reviews, comparison notes, and review checklist
- `docs/summaries/`: executive/package summaries and planning notes
- `scripts/`: utility scripts for figures and local task helpers
- `artifacts/build/`: LaTeX build byproducts
- `artifacts/exports/`: exported PDF/DOCX deliverables
- `artifacts/source_images/`: source copies of diagram images
- `artifacts/runtime_screenshots/`: runtime screenshot captures
